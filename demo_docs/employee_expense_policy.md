# Employee Travel and Expense Policy

International flight bookings require VP approval at least 14 days before departure.

Daily meal reimbursement is capped at **$75 per day** for domestic travel and **$110 per day** for international travel.

Expense receipts over **$25** must be uploaded to the finance portal within **21 days** of trip completion.

Any expense report exceeding **$500** requires approval from the department director before reimbursement.

Mileage reimbursement for approved business travel is calculated at **$0.65 per mile**.
