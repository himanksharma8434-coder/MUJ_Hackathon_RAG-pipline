Students may borrow up to **5 books** at one time.

The standard borrowing period is **14 days**. Students may renew a book once for an additional 7 days if nobody else has requested it.

Books returned late incur a fine of **$1 per day** per book, capped at **$15**.

The library is open from **8:00 AM to 10:00 PM** on weekdays and from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekends.

Students must present their university ID when borrowing or returning books.
