# Enterprise Cloud Service-Level Agreement

## Availability and service credits

Production database clusters guarantee **99.95% monthly uptime**, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance.

If monthly uptime falls below 99.95%, customers receive a **15% service credit** on the affected monthly bill.

Critical Severity-1 incidents require an initial engineering response within **15 minutes** and automated failover recovery within **120 seconds**.

Encrypted backups use AES-256 and are retained for **35 days** in geo-redundant storage across Frankfurt and Dublin.

## Support requirements

Customers must include the affected service, incident time, and request identifier when opening an SLA claim. Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the incident.
