Campus Hostel Rules

Hostel quiet hours are from **10:00 PM to 6:00 AM** every day.

Visitors must sign in at the hostel reception and leave the building by **8:00 PM**.

Residents must report maintenance problems through the hostel portal within 24 hours of discovering them.

Cooking with open flames is prohibited inside student rooms.

Residents must keep their room keys secure and report a lost key to the warden immediately.
