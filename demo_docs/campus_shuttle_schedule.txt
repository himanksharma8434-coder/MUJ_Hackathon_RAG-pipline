Campus Shuttle Service

Campus shuttles operate from **7:00 AM to 10:00 PM** on weekdays.

During normal service, a shuttle arrives approximately every **20 minutes**.

Students must show their university ID when boarding after 8:00 PM.

The north route connects the hostel, library, student center, and main gate.

The south route connects the academic blocks, sports complex, cafeteria, and main gate.
